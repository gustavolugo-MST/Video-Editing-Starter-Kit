## Interview (Claude asks these one at a time)

1. What is your brand or channel name, and what do you do in one sentence?
2. Who is your ideal viewer? (age, job, what they struggle with)
3. What do you want viewers to do after watching? (follow, subscribe, call, click a link)
4. How should you sound? Pick 3 words (for example: friendly, direct, funny, calm, expert).
5. Which 2-3 brands or creators do you like the look of, and what do you like about them?
6. Do you have a logo, colors, or fonts already? If yes, share them.
7. Light or dark look? Bold and loud, or clean and simple?
8. Are there words, topics, or styles to always avoid?
